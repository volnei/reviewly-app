The centerpiece

It reads the whole PR.
Then it walks you through it.

Most tools dump a flat list of files on you. Reviewly's AI plans a route — orientation first, then the risky bits, then a quick nod to what's solid — so a 40-file diff feels like a guided tour instead of an archaeology dig.

  • Risk-ordered stopsEach stop is tagged — orientation, worth a comment, question, or nice — so you spend attention where it matters.
  • Drafted comments, your callEvery concern comes with a comment you can edit, add to your review, or post straight to GitHub — or skip.
  • Runs on your machineIt drives your local Claude or Codex CLI. The diff is read locally — nothing is shipped to a third-party reviewer.
