What it does

Less tab-soup. More reviewing.

Everything you need to actually read a pull request, in one quiet, keyboard-first window.

01

Guided AI review

A risk-ordered walkthrough — orientation, the parts worth a comment, then a nod to what's solid — with editable comments you add to your review or post straight to GitHub.

02

Your local git workspace

Clone and associate repos, check out any PR with one click, then stage, commit (with an AI-drafted message), and push — without leaving the app.

03

Calm & keyboard-first

A Linear-clean interface with a command palette, j/k navigation, split & unified diffs, and mark-viewed-and-next. No spinners between every click.

04

Local & private

Your token and code stay on your machine. AI review drives the Claude or Codex CLI you already have installed — nothing is uploaded to a third-party reviewer.
